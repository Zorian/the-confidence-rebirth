You asked, Dani answered

Is this only about body confidence?

No. Body is one part. We work on confidence at work, in relationships, with money, travelling and living on your own terms.

Where does everything happen?

In our private community app. Live calls, replays and the chat are all in one place, on your phone or laptop.

What if I can't make the live calls?

Every call is recorded. Free members get the replay for 7 days. Club members and above keep every replay.

Which option is right for me?

Start with the free workshop. If you want ongoing support, join the Club. If you want Dani in your corner every week, apply for the Circle or VIP.

Can I cancel the Club?

Yes, any time, in two taps. You keep access until the end of the month you paid for.

What happens on the call?

A relaxed 20 minute chat about where you are and where you want to be. If the Circle or VIP is right for you, we'll tell you. If it isn't, we'll point you to what is. No pressure.

Are there payment plans?

Yes. The Circle and VIP both have payment plans. We'll go through the options on your call.

How do I contact Dani?

Send a DM to @theconfidencerebirth on Instagram. We reply within a day.
