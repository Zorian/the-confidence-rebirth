Play button options

Six ways to show the play button without covering Dani's face. Each one runs the real preview clip, so you can see how it looks as it moves.

1

Tap to unmute MY PICK

Same as Instagram and TikTok. The video already looks like it's playing, so people tap for the sound.

2

Yellow watch bar

A full strip along the bottom in the brand yellow. Easy to spot, and it shows how long the video is.

3

Play on the bookshelf side

Keeps the classic round button but moves it to the empty space beside her.

4

Start here pill

Merges the play button and "Start here" into one tidy pill in the corner. Cleanest look.

5

Handwritten note

A pen note with an arrow to a yellow play button. Fits the notebook feel of the site.

6

Title along the bottom

A soft dark fade at the bottom with the play button and a title. Feels like a proper film.
