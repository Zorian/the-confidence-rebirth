Your rebirth in 5 steps

The same 5 pillars Dani takes every woman through, whatever area of life you want your confidence back in.

Step1

Reawaken

Notice what’s really been running your life.

  • Hear the voice that keeps telling you to play small
  • Spot where you shrink, people please and hide
  • Remember who you were before the doubt crept in
Step2

Repair

Heal the places it all started.

  • Unpack the moments that taught you to dim yourself
  • Let go of old comments, comparison and hurt
  • Make peace with your past so it stops steering
Step3

Rewire

Change the story you tell yourself.

  • Swap the beliefs that keep you stuck for ones that back you
  • Build an inner voice that talks to you like a best friend
  • Practise new thoughts until they feel natural
Step4

Refocus

Put your energy where it grows you.

  • Handle triggers, bad days and setbacks without spiralling
  • Stop comparing and start choosing you
  • Set boundaries that protect your peace
Step5

Rebuild

Show up as the woman you’re becoming.

  • Walk into any room like you belong there
  • Speak up at work, in love and online
  • Keep your confidence for life, not just for a week

Is this for everyone?

No, and that's on purpose. It's for the woman who is ready to do the work. If you're not sure yet, start with the free workshop and see how it feels.

Is this only about body confidence?

No. Body is one part. We work on confidence at work, in relationships, with money, travelling and living on your own terms.

Where does everything happen?

In our private community app. Live calls, replays and the chat are all in one place, on your phone or laptop.

What if I can't make the live calls?

Every call is recorded. Free members get the replay for 7 days. Masterclass and 1:1 women keep every replay.

Which option is right for me?

Start with the free workshop. If you want to go at your own pace, get the course. If you want Dani in your corner every week, apply for the masterclass or 1:1.

How do I know which method is mine?

Take the 2 minute quiz. It points you to the one that fits where you are right now.

Why is only one method open?

We fill one group, then open the next. If yours isn't open yet, join the waitlist and you'll hear first.

What happens on the call?

A relaxed 20 minute chat about where you are and where you want to be. If the masterclass or 1:1 is right for you, we'll tell you. If it isn't, we'll point you to what is. No pressure.

Are there payment plans?

Yes. The masterclass and 1:1 both have payment plans. We'll go through the options on your call.

How do I contact Dani?

Send a DM to @theconfidencerebirth on Instagram. We reply within a day.
