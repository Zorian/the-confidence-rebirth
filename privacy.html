Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

The short version. We only collect what you give us on our forms, plus basic site analytics. We use it to run the community, the workshops and coaching, and to keep in touch by email and WhatsApp if you said yes. We never sell your details. You can ask us to stop or delete them at any time by emailing theconfidencerebirth@gmail.com.

Who we are

The Confidence Rebirth is Dani Carbonari's confidence coaching community. In this policy, "we" and "us" means The Confidence Rebirth. For anything about your data, email theconfidencerebirth@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Details you give us on our forms: your full name, email address, WhatsApp number and, if you add it, your Instagram handle.
  • Your answers: the method you choose, your answers on the coaching application, and anything you tell us you want help with.
  • Quiz results: your Confidence Type is worked out in your browser. We don't store your quiz answers against your name.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We see that you paid and what for, but never your full card details.
  • Calls: if you book a call, Calendly passes us the name, email and answers you give when booking.
  • Site analytics: Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel record how the site is used, such as pages viewed and buttons clicked, using cookies and similar technology.

How we use it

  • To add you to the course waitlist and tell you when your course opens, with the founding price.
  • To review your coaching application, take payment and get you started.
  • To invite you to the free community and remind you about live workshops.
  • To send you updates and offers by email and WhatsApp, if you ticked the box to say you're happy for us to.
  • To understand what's working on the site and to show our ads to people likely to find them useful.

Our legal reasons for using your data are your consent (for marketing and analytics), taking the steps you asked for before a contract and then delivering it (for applications and coaching), and our legitimate interest in running and improving our community.

Who we share it with

We use trusted services to run The Confidence Rebirth, and they only use your data to provide their service to us:

  • Google (Sheets, to keep our list of members and applications, and Analytics)
  • Circle (our community platform)
  • Stripe (payments) and Calendly (call booking)
  • Meta (the Meta Pixel, Instagram and WhatsApp)

Some of these services store data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, they use approved safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. We never sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while you're part of the community or on a waitlist, and for up to 2 years after we last heard from you. Payment records are kept for 6 years because the law requires it. After that we delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for marketing. You can also withdraw your consent at any time, by replying STOP on WhatsApp, using the unsubscribe link in an email, or emailing theconfidencerebirth@gmail.com. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We'd love the chance to put it right first, so please talk to us.

Cookies

We use cookies for analytics and advertising through Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page.
